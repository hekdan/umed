Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

The short version: what you enter into üMed, including any photos, stays on your device. We do not collect, store, sell, or share your health information, because we never receive it. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

This policy covers the üMed website at hekdan.github.io/umed (the “app”), published by üMed (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no server and no database. It does not ask for your name, email, or date of birth, and it contains no third-party scripts, trackers, or advertising tools.

How your answers are handled

  • Your answers, including anything you type in your own words, are processed only in your browser’s memory to show your result.
  • They are cleared when you close or reload the page. Choosing “Start over” clears them too. Two things are kept in memory until you reload: the “Who is this for?” choice, and any health-history questions you have already answered, such as whether you take a blood thinner.
  • They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Photos

  • If you add photos of the area of concern, your phone’s own camera or photo picker provides them. The app never turns on the camera by itself.
  • Your browser resizes each photo and saves it again as a new image. This removes location (GPS) data and other hidden details about the original file.
  • Photos are kept only in memory and are never uploaded. They go only into the report or electronic-record export that you choose to download. Choosing “Start over” or reloading the page discards them.

What is stored on your device

  • Your acceptance of the Terms. One setting (umed.ack) records the version of the Terms you accepted, so the app does not ask again. It contains no health or personal information.
  • App files for offline use. Your browser caches the app’s own files (pages, fonts, icons) so it works offline. The cache holds no personal information.

You can delete both at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Cookies

üMed uses no cookies of any kind. There are no advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies either. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Deleting your data

We hold no information about you, so there is nothing for us to access, export, correct, or delete. Anything üMed keeps lives only on your device:

  • To erase it, tap “Erase üMed data on this device” at the bottom of any screen. This clears your answers, photos, your acceptance of the Terms, and the offline copy of the app.
  • You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • Reports you downloaded are ordinary files on your device. Delete them the way you would any other file.

Reports, notes, and exports

The visit summary (PDF), the note you can copy for your chart, and the electronic-record export (FHIR) are created on your device. They are saved, or copied to your clipboard, only when you choose to do so. They contain your health information, so keep them somewhere private. Once you give them to a clinic or another person, that recipient’s own privacy practices apply.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver and protect the site, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs. The app is always served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Links to other sites

When you tap “Read more on MedlinePlus”, your browser opens the U.S. National Library of Medicine’s website, and the condition name is part of that link. Opening it is your choice. The app tells your browser not to send our page’s address with the request. MedlinePlus has its own privacy policy.

HIPAA

HIPAA applies to health care providers, health plans, and their business associates. üMed is not a covered entity or business associate and receives no protected health information. HIPAA therefore does not apply to your use of the app. When you share a report with your clinician, their HIPAA obligations apply to the copy they receive.

State consumer health data laws

Some states, such as Washington (My Health My Data Act), Nevada, and Connecticut, give you rights over “consumer health data”. We do not collect, share, or sell consumer health data, and we hold none about you. There is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, or disclose.

Security and breach notification

Because we hold no identifiable information about you, there is nothing on our side that could be breached. The protections built into the app:

  • a strict content security policy, so the app runs only its own code
  • no third-party code when you use it
  • a PDF library that is pinned to a reviewed version and integrity-checked

The security of anything stored on your device is up to you, through your device’s own settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian may use it on a child’s behalf. We collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we ever change how information is handled, for example by adding a feature that sends data anywhere, we will update this policy first. If the change is material, the app will ask you to accept it before you continue.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/hekdan/umed. Please do not include personal or health information. Issues there are public.
