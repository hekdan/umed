Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version: your yes/no answers and any photos stay on your device. Two things can leave it: in editions with the reading step, the words you type are sent once, automatically, to an AI reading service that keeps nothing (see “The words you type”); and an optional photo second opinion sends a photo only when you tap yes (see “Photos”). We do not collect, store, sell, or share your health information. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

The üMed iPhone app (App Store edition): it has no reading step and no photo second opinion, and it is blocked from making any network connection except to its own built-in files. Nothing you type, tap, or photograph leaves your phone. The “Hosting” section below applies only to the website.

This policy covers the üMed website at hekdan.github.io/umed (the “app”), published by üMed (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

Nothing is collected or kept. The app has no database and no accounts. Its only servers are the reading step described under “The words you type” and the optional photo second opinion described under “Photos”; both keep nothing. It does not ask for your name, email, or date of birth, and it contains no third-party scripts, trackers, or advertising tools.

How your answers are handled

  • Your answers, including anything you type in your own words, are processed only in your browser’s memory to show your result.
  • They are cleared when you close or reload the page. Choosing “Start over” clears them too. Three things are kept in memory until you reload: the “Who is this for?” choice; any health-history questions you have already answered, such as whether you take a blood thinner; and the advice from a recent check, with its time (such as “Get seen today”), so a new check can remind you of it. None of your words are kept with it.
  • Your yes/no answers and health-history answers are never sent to us or to anyone else. The words you type are, in editions with the reading step; the next section says how.

The words you type

  • Some editions of the app include a reading step: when you tap Continue, the words you typed are sent once, automatically, over an encrypted connection, to üMed’s reading service, which runs an AI model on Cloudflare, with your age group and sex. The model returns a short list of likely causes and an urgency, which the app blends into its own result. There is no separate consent screen for this; the “Please read this first” screen tells you before you start, and using the app with that screen accepted is your agreement to it.
  • Nothing else travels with the words: no name, account, email, phone identifier, photo, or yes/no answer. We do not store the words or the model’s answer. Cloudflare says it does not keep the words after the reading, unless the law requires it, and does not train AI on them.
  • Free text can still identify a person. Please do not type names, addresses, dates of birth, or other details that identify you or anyone else; the app does not need them. If you prefer that nothing you type leaves your device, use an edition without the reading step, or do not use the app.
  • If the reading server cannot be reached, the app gives its result from your words and answers alone and says so on the result screen.
  • If your edition has the reading step, the “Please read this first” screen says “What you type is read by an AI service.” If it says “Your answers and photos stay on this device,” your edition has no reading step and nothing you type leaves your device.

Photos

  • If you add photos of the area of concern, your phone’s own camera or photo picker provides them. The app never turns on the camera by itself.
  • Your browser resizes each photo and saves it again as a new image. This removes location (GPS) data and other hidden details about the original file.
  • If you add a photo of a rash, a small model inside the app looks at it on your device to suggest likely causes and to choose which picture question to show first. This happens in your browser; the photo is not sent anywhere for it.
  • Some editions of the app offer an optional second opinion on the photo. Nothing is sent unless you tap “Yes, send this photo” on that screen. If you do, the photo and the words you typed are sent once, over an encrypted connection, to üMed’s photo server and on to Anthropic (the maker of the Claude AI model), which returns a description of what the photo looks like. We do not store the photo or the words, no account or name travels with them, and Anthropic’s API terms do not allow it to train on them. If that screen is not shown in your edition, no photo ever leaves your device.
  • Photos are kept only in memory and are never uploaded without that tap. They go only into the report or electronic-record export that you choose to download. Choosing “Start over” or reloading the page discards them.

What is stored on your device

Your answers and photos are never saved. üMed keeps one small note on your device: that you tapped “I understand” before you started, so the notice is not shown every time. It contains no personal information. üMed does not keep an offline copy of itself. Like any website, your browser may keep copies of the app’s own files (pages, fonts, icons) in its usual cache. They contain no personal information either.

Cookies

üMed uses no cookies of any kind. There are no advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies either. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Deleting your data

We hold no information about you, so there is nothing for us to access, export, correct, or delete. Anything üMed keeps lives only on your device:

  • To erase it, tap “Erase data” at the bottom of any screen. This clears your answers, your photos and the “I understand” note.
  • You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • Reports you downloaded are ordinary files on your device. Delete them the way you would any other file.

Reports, notes, and exports

The visit summary (PDF), the note you can copy for your chart, and the electronic-record export (FHIR) are created on your device. They are saved, or copied to your clipboard, only when you choose to do so. They contain your health information, so keep them somewhere private. Once you give them to a clinic or another person, that recipient’s own privacy practices apply. The optional “Remind me” calendar file is also created on your device; it holds a date and a generic check-back line, none of your words or answers.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver and protect the site, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs. The app is always served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Links to other sites

When you tap “Read more on MedlinePlus”, your browser opens the U.S. National Library of Medicine’s website, and the condition name is part of that link. Opening it is your choice. The app tells your browser not to send our page’s address with the request. MedlinePlus has its own privacy policy.

HIPAA

HIPAA applies to health care providers, health plans, and their business associates. üMed is not a covered entity or business associate and receives no protected health information. HIPAA therefore does not apply to your use of the app. When you share a report with your clinician, their HIPAA obligations apply to the copy they receive.

State consumer health data laws

Some states, such as Washington (My Health My Data Act), Nevada, and Connecticut, give you rights over “consumer health data”. We do not collect, share, or sell consumer health data, and we hold none about you. There is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, or disclose.

Security and breach notification

Because we hold no identifiable information about you, there is nothing on our side that could be breached. The protections built into the app:

  • a strict content security policy, so the app runs only its own code
  • no third-party code when you use it; the reading step (in editions that have it) and the optional photo second opinion are the only outside connections, both to servers run for üMed
  • a PDF library that is pinned to a reviewed version and integrity-checked

The security of anything stored on your device is up to you, through your device’s own settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian may use it on a child’s behalf. We collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we ever change how information is handled, for example by adding a feature that sends data anywhere, we will update this policy first. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/hekdan/umed. Please do not include personal or health information. Issues there are public.
