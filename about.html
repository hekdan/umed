About üMed

Updated October 18, 2026

üMed is a free, educational symptom-intake tool. It helps you organize your symptoms and prepare a summary for your own clinician. It is not medical advice, and it is not for emergencies: in an emergency, call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988.

Who publishes üMed

  • Publisher: üMed, Indiana, United States
  • Website: hekdan.github.io/umed, hosted on GitHub Pages
  • Price: free. No purchases, subscriptions, advertising, or sponsorships.
  • Contact: open an issue at github.com/hekdan/umed. Please do not include personal or health information, because issues there are public.

Accessibility

We want üMed to be usable by everyone, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA. Current measures:

  • Text and buttons meet AA color-contrast ratios in light and dark mode.
  • Every control can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline. In the app, a “Skip to main content” link is the first item.
  • On each new screen, focus moves to its heading, so screen readers announce where you are.
  • Answer options and main buttons are at least 44 pixels tall.
  • Images carry text alternatives.
  • The layout respects your device’s text size, dark mode, and reduced-motion settings.

If something gets in your way, please tell us through the contact above. We will work to fix it.
